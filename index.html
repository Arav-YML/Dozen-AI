1 Overview

Dozen AI is a native Android application designed for app testers and developers who need to track and maintain consistent daily usage of multiple testing applications. It organises tracked apps into named groups (e.g., Alpha, Beta, or any custom name), maintains a day counter per group, sends daily reminders, generates AI-powered feedback text for each app, and checks for app updates on the Google Play Store.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Dozen AI collects, why it collects it, how it is stored, and what choices you have. By installing and using Dozen AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the application.

Dozen AI does NOT require account registration. We do NOT collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. All core app data — tracked apps, groups, day counts, feedback — is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any developer-owned server.

3 How We Use Your Information

Information handled by Dozen AI is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Core tracking functionality — storing your tracked app entries, group assignments, day counts, and usage history locally on your device so you can monitor your testing regularity.
  • Daily reminders — using locally stored reminder toggle states to schedule morning, afternoon, and night notification alarms at the correct times via Android's alarm system, without any server involvement.
  • AI feedback generation — using Google sign-in for generating feedback of Apps to generate and store feedback text locally on your device.
  • App version checking — checking publicly available version information to compare against installed app versions. No user data is transmitted.
  • Serving advertisements — the Google AdMob SDK uses your Advertising ID to display banner, interstitial, app open, native, and rewarded advertisements. Ad revenue helps keep the app free.
  • Rewarded ad gate — a daily counter (stored in SharedPreferences) tracks how many times you have used the "Get Feedback" feature today. After 2 free uses, you may watch a rewarded ad to unlock additional uses. This counter is stored entirely on-device.
We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with any third party other than Google AdMob (via its SDK) as described above. We operate no backend server that receives any app usage data from your device.

4 Data Storage & Retention

On-Device SQLite Database (Room): All tracked app records and usage history are stored in a local SQLite database on your device managed by Android Room. The schema contains two tables: tracked_apps (app entries, groups, day counts) and app_history (date-level usage records). This data never leaves your device.

On-Device SharedPreferences: Reminder settings, day-start timestamps, onboarding completion flag, and cached Play Store version data (version strings only) are stored in Android SharedPreferences. No personal data is included.

On-Device Files: Generated feedback text (up to 16 sentences per tracked app, per group) is stored as text data in the app's private file storage. This data is only accessible to the app and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

AdMob / Google: The Google AdMob SDK transmits your Advertising ID and ad interaction data to Google's servers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. This data is retained by Google under their own policies, which Dozen AI does not control.

Retention: All on-device data is retained until you uninstall the app. There is no automatic expiry. No developer-side server retains any user data.

6 Advertising (AdMob)

Dozen AI is a free app supported by advertising through Google AdMob. The following ad formats are used:

Banner Interstitial App Open Native Rewarded Video

  • Banner ads appear at the bottom of the main screen, group room, and feedback viewer.
  • Interstitial ads appear before the Feedback Viewer opens.
  • App Open ads appear on cold start (with a 10-minute cooldown between shows).
  • Native ads appear inline after every 3 group tiles on the main screen, and after every 4 feedback day-cards in the Feedback Viewer.
  • Rewarded video ads are optional — you may watch one to unlock additional "Get Feedback" uses after your 2 free daily uses are exhausted.

AdMob may use your Advertising ID (GAID) to show personalised ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  1. Open your device Settings
  2. Tap Privacy (or Google → Ads)
  3. Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"

You can also reset your Advertising ID from the same screen to generate a fresh anonymous identifier unlinked from any previous ad history.

The app does not show ads during active feedback generation or during Google sign-in for generating feedback of Apps, to avoid interfering with critical user flows.

7 AI Feedback Feature

The "Get Feedback" feature generates 16 day-wise feedback sentences for each tracked app using AI. Here is the data flow — every step is transparent:

  1. Google sign-in for generating feedback of Apps: You sign in with your own Google account inside the app. The app does not read, store, or transmit your password, recovery information, or any account credentials.
  2. Prompt submission: The app submits an AI feedback prompt on your behalf using your authenticated session. No personal information is included in the prompt.
  3. Storage of response: The 16 feedback sentences returned by the AI are stored locally on your device in the app's private file storage. They are never uploaded to any server.
The app does not log, transmit, or store your Google account credentials, password, or recovery information. Your Google sign-in session for generating feedback of Apps is managed entirely on-device and is not accessible to any external party through this app.

8 App Permissions

Dozen AI requests the following Android permissions. Each permission is used only for the specific purpose described:

Permission Why It Is Needed
INTERNET Required for AdMob ads, app version checking, and Google sign-in for generating feedback of Apps.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks whether the device has an active network connection before making any network request.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (API 33+) Displays daily reminder notifications (morning, afternoon, night) for tracked app usage. Requested at runtime — you can deny or revoke this permission at any time in Android Settings.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores scheduled reminder alarms after the device is restarted. Without this, reminders would stop working after a reboot.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Runs a foreground service that monitors app usage tracking in the background with a persistent notification, as required by Android policy.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC Declares the foreground service type for Android 14+ compliance.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Prompts you to exclude Dozen AI from battery optimisation so that reminders fire reliably at the correct time rather than being deferred by Android's Doze mode.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM Schedules precise alarm times for morning, afternoon, and night reminder notifications so they fire exactly when configured.
WAKE_LOCK Required internally by AndroidX WorkManager to keep the CPU awake long enough to complete background reminder scheduling tasks.
VIBRATE Enables vibration for reminder notifications.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Required by the Google AdMob SDK to access the Android Advertising ID for ad personalisation. You can reset or opt out of this in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Dozen AI does not request permissions for camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, phone state, or any storage access beyond its own private app directory.

10 Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over the data Dozen AI stores on your device:

  • Delete all app data: Uninstall the app from your device. This permanently removes all tracked app records, usage history, preferences, and generated feedback text. Alternatively, go to Android Settings → Apps → Dozen AI → Storage → "Clear Data".
  • Revoke notification permission: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Dozen AI → Notifications → toggle off. This disables all reminder notifications but does not affect tracking or feedback functionality.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: Go to Android Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalisation". Non-personalised ads will still be shown.
  • Reset your Advertising ID: Go to Android Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → "Reset Advertising ID" to unlink your device from any previous ad-personalisation history.
  • Sign out of Google Account (AI Feedback): You can sign out of the Google account used for generating feedback of Apps from within the app's sign-in settings, or by clearing app data via Android Settings → Apps → Dozen AI → Storage → "Clear Data".
  • Disable battery optimisation exemption: If you previously granted this, you can revoke it via Android Settings → Apps → Dozen AI → Battery → "Optimised". Reminders may become less reliable after doing so.

11 Data Security

We apply the following security measures to protect information handled by Dozen AI:

  • All network communications — AdMob requests, version checking, Google sign-in for generating feedback of Apps — use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • The app operates with no developer-owned backend server. There is no database, API endpoint, or cloud function that receives your data. This means there is no server-side data to breach.
  • On-device data (Room database, SharedPreferences, feedback files) is stored in the app's private storage, which is not accessible to other apps on a non-rooted device.

No method of data storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data handled by third-party SDKs (AdMob, WebView) which are governed by their own security practices.
