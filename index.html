1 Overview

Dozen AI is a native Android application designed for app testers and developers who need to track and maintain consistent daily usage of multiple testing applications. It organises tracked apps into named groups (e.g., Alpha, Beta, or any custom name), maintains a day counter per group, sends daily reminders, generates AI-powered feedback text for each app, and checks for app updates on the Google Play Store.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Dozen AI collects, why it collects it, how it is stored, and what choices you have. By installing and using Dozen AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the application.

Dozen AI does NOT require account registration. We do NOT collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. Core app data — tracked apps, groups, day counts, generated feedback — is stored on your device. The app transmits limited, anonymous device-level analytics to developer-owned Firebase services (described fully in Sections 2–5). No data that could directly identify you as an individual is ever collected.

3 How We Use Your Information

Information handled by Dozen AI is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Core tracking functionality — storing your tracked app entries, group assignments, day counts, and usage history locally on your device.
  • Daily reminders — using locally stored reminder toggle states to schedule morning, afternoon, and night notification alarms via Android's alarm system.
  • AI feedback generation — using Google sign-in for generating feedback of Apps to generate and store feedback text locally on your device.
  • App version checking — checking publicly available Play Store version information. No user data is transmitted.
  • Serving advertisements — the Google AdMob SDK uses your Advertising ID to display banner, interstitial, app open, native, and rewarded advertisements. Ad revenue helps keep the app free.
  • Rewarded ad gate — a daily counter (stored in SharedPreferences) tracks "Get Feedback" uses. After 2 free uses, you may watch a rewarded ad for additional uses.
  • Developer analytics (Firebase) — anonymous device-level data (install heartbeat, feature usage counts, app version distribution) is sent to the developer's Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Analytics to understand app health, engagement, and adoption. No PII is included. This data is used solely by the developer for app improvement.
We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with any third party for commercial purposes. Firebase Realtime Database data is stored in the developer's own project and is not accessible to other third parties. Firebase Analytics data is processed by Google under their own Privacy Policy.

4 Data Storage & Retention

On-Device SQLite Database (Room): All tracked app records and usage history are stored in a local SQLite database managed by Android Room. This data never leaves your device.

On-Device SharedPreferences: Reminder settings, day-start timestamps, onboarding completion flag, and cached Play Store version data are stored in Android SharedPreferences. No personal data is included.

On-Device Files: Generated feedback text (up to 16 sentences per tracked app, per group) is stored in the app's private file storage. Deleted on uninstall.

Firebase Realtime Database (Developer-Owned): Anonymous device analytics data (install heartbeat, feedback usage counts) are transmitted to the developer's Firebase Realtime Database. Data is stored keyed by Android ID (hex). The developer retains this data indefinitely for analytics purposes. Firebase's infrastructure is operated by Google.

Firebase Analytics (Google): Standard and custom analytics events are processed by Firebase Analytics. Data is retained by Google under their default Analytics retention settings (up to 14 months). See Google's Privacy Policy.

AdMob / Google: The Google AdMob SDK transmits your Advertising ID and ad interaction data to Google's servers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Retention of on-device data: Retained until you uninstall the app or clear app data. Firebase RTDB data is retained by the developer indefinitely but contains no PII and cannot be linked back to an individual without the device.

6 Advertising (AdMob)

Dozen AI is a free app supported by advertising through Google AdMob. The following ad formats are used:

Banner Interstitial App Open Native Rewarded Video

  • Banner ads appear at the bottom of the main screen, group room, and feedback viewer.
  • Interstitial ads appear before the Feedback Viewer opens.
  • App Open ads appear on cold start (10-minute cooldown between shows).
  • Native ads appear inline after every 3 group tiles on the main screen, and after every 4 feedback day-cards in the Feedback Viewer.
  • Rewarded video ads are optional — watch one to unlock additional "Get Feedback" uses after your 2 free daily uses are exhausted.

AdMob may use your Advertising ID (GAID) to show personalised ads. You can opt out at any time:

  1. Open device Settings
  2. Tap Privacy (or Google → Ads)
  3. Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"
The app does not show ads during active feedback generation or during Google sign-in for generating feedback of Apps.

7 AI Feedback Feature

The "Get Feedback" feature generates 16 day-wise feedback sentences for each tracked app using AI. Data flow:

  1. Google sign-in for generating feedback of Apps: You sign in with your own Google account inside the app. The app does not read, store, or transmit your password, recovery information, or any account credentials.
  2. Prompt submission: The app submits an AI feedback prompt on your behalf using your authenticated session. No personal information is included in the prompt.
  3. Storage of response: The 16 feedback sentences are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to any server.
  4. Usage analytics: After each "Get Feedback" use, an anonymous event is sent to Firebase RTDB and Firebase Analytics recording: group name (user-chosen label), number of apps in the group, and whether the daily free-use limit was exceeded. No app names, package names, or feedback content is transmitted.
The app does not log, transmit, or store your Google account credentials, password, or recovery information. Your sign-in session is managed entirely on-device.

10 Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over the data Dozen AI stores:

  • Delete all on-device data: Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Dozen AI → Storage → "Clear Data".
  • Opt out of Firebase analytics: Firebase analytics collection is tied to the app. To stop it, uninstall the app. There is no in-app toggle, as the data collected is anonymous and non-PII.
  • Revoke notification permission: Android Settings → Apps → Dozen AI → Notifications → toggle off.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".
  • Reset your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → "Reset Advertising ID".
  • Delete Feedback Data: Contact us at the email below to request deletion of your Feedback Feature usage records from Firebase. We will process your request within 30 days.
  • Sign out of Google Account (AI Feedback): From within the app's sign-in settings, or by clearing app data.
  • Disable battery optimisation exemption: Android Settings → Apps → Dozen AI → Battery → "Optimised".

11 Data Security

We apply the following security measures:

  • All network communications — AdMob requests, Firebase RTDB writes, Firebase Analytics events, version checking, Google sign-in — use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Firebase RTDB security rules enforce write-only access from devices (no device can read back any other device's data) and validate all field types and sizes before accepting writes.
  • On-device data (Room database, SharedPreferences, feedback files) is stored in the app's private storage, inaccessible to other apps on a non-rooted device.
No method of data storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data handled by third-party SDKs (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, WebView) which are governed by their own security practices. Firebase RTDB stores only anonymous, non-PII data, minimising the impact of any theoretical breach.
